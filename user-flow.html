The DFM Workflow

The DFM Issue is the
atomic unit of work.

Chorus replaces the CAD → PowerPoint → email loop with a GitHub-Issues workflow for manufacturability. Every manufacturing risk becomes a tracked, stateful issue — raised on a revision by a provider, decided by the brand, implemented in a new revision, validated by the reviewer, and closed. A part is cleared to cut steel — DFM Approved — when every issue is dispositioned and the joint sign-offs are signed.

Package→Issues→Disposition→Validation→Sign-off→DFM Approval

1 The atomic unit

The unit of work is a single DFM Issue — one manufacturability concern, raised against one revision of one part by one provider, carried until it's validated and closed. Issues now carry a Type (show-stopper · finding · proposal · info) that routes them differently, and the terminal gate is DFM Approval — every issue dispositioned + joint sign-offs signed = cut steel.

3 Issue lifecycle · state machine

Open → Dispositioned → Implemented → Validated → Closed
Reject pathOpen→Dispositioned · rejected→Closed— terminal · rationale required ◆
Clarify loopOpen→Needs clarification→Open— reviewer answers, issue re-opens
Failed validationImplemented→Validation failed→Open— fix didn't hold, back to the brand
Roll-up gate ◆ — a single issue closes here, but the part only reaches DFM Approved when every issue is dispositioned/closed and all joint sign-offs are signed. At that moment the approved revision is frozen and steel can be cut.

4 The core flow · 11 steps

1
Create Project + PartsBrand
One project, one or more parts — each can be DFM'd by multiple providers.
2
Upload PackageBrand
STEP · 2D+GD&T · material spec · cosmetic grades · volume — Rev A.
3
Package CompleteGate ◆
The package must meet the process-type checklist before reviewers can be invited — bad inputs were the #1 cause of useless DFM feedback.
4
Invite Reviewer(s)Brand
NDA gate, then a scoped magic link per provider — each starts its own DFM.
5
Review PartReviewer
Raises typed issues — show-stopper · finding · proposal · info.
6
Disposition IssuesBrand
Accept · reject (rationale required) · clarify. Issue Groups roll up cross-provider duplicates with a conflict flag.
7
Sign-offsJoint ◆
Parting line, gate location, material lock — both parties sign. First-class, not issues.
8
Upload New RevisionBrand
Implements accepted changes; each DFM's review pointer advances at its own pace.
9
Validate FixesReviewer
The reviewer who raised the issue re-confirms — created-on rev vs fixed-in rev.
10
Close IssuesSystem
Validated issues close; failed validations reopen.
11
DFM Approval — cut steelJoint ◆
All issues dispositioned + sign-offs signed → freeze the approved revision → steel can be cut. This is the moment value is realized.

5 Two actors, one source of truth

AL
Brand
ALSO · owns design intent
  • Creates projects, parts, and uploads revisions
  • Makes the decision on every issue — accept, reject, clarify
  • Implements accepted fixes in a new revision
  • Cannot validate its own fixes
HP
External Reviewer · one per provider
Hsinchu Precision · owns manufacturability
  • Reviews revisions and raises typed DFM issues with recommendations
  • Validates the fixes to its own issues by comparing revisions
  • Co-signs the joint sign-offs — parting line, gate, material
  • NDA-gated, watermarked, and walled off from other providers' DFMs

6 What's new in v2 · the six fixes ◆

1
Package gate
Reviewers can't be invited until the OEM package meets the process-type checklist. Bad inputs were the #1 cause of useless DFM feedback.
2
Issue Type axis
Show-stoppers, findings, proposals, and info notes flow through disposition differently — not everything is a blocking "must fix."
3
Per-DFM revision pointer
Each provider reviews at its own pace — Provider 1 can be on Rev B while Provider 2 is still on Rev A. Revisions are shared; review state isn't.
4
Issue Groups
The brand dispositions one cross-provider duplicate once, with a conflict flag when providers disagree. One unified decision cascades to every linked DFM.
5
Sign-offs
Parting line, gate location, and material lock are joint agreements between named parties — first-class objects, not issues one side "accepts."
6
DFM Approval gate
The terminal state is the steel-cut decision: all issues dispositioned + sign-offs signed + the approved revision frozen. This replaces the vague "DFM Complete."
